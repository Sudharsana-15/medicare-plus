Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and policies.

How do I book an appointment?

You can easily book an appointment through our online portal on the "Appointments" page, by calling our helpline, or by visiting the hospital reception directly.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept a wide range of major health insurance providers. Please contact our billing department or check the insurance section for a comprehensive list.

What are the standard visiting hours?

General ward visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. ICU visiting hours are strictly restricted and coordinated by the nursing staff.

Do you offer telemedicine consultations?

Yes, we offer secure video consultations with our specialists. You can choose the telemedicine option while booking your appointment online.

How can I access my medical records?

Once registered, you can access your lab reports, prescriptions, and medical history through our secure patient portal by logging into your account.

What should I bring for my first visit?

Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, any past medical records, a list of current medications, and a referral letter if applicable.

Need Immediate Medical Assistance?

Our emergency department and specialists are ready to help you 24/7.
